# HRE imperial military access

The Holy Roman Empire has a zero-influence, mutual military-access treaty article between its current leader and each member. It uses the game's `is_military_access` treaty flag, because no power bloc modifier grants land military access. Starting with Austria and 68 members, this needs 68 treaties instead of thousands of member-to-member treaties.

The treaty article is valid only while both countries share the HRE power bloc and one is its leader. A startup effect creates missing treaties; a monthly leader pulse adds treaties for new members or a new leader. If membership or leadership changes, invalid articles expire through their maintenance requirement. The article cannot be proposed or withdrawn through ordinary diplomacy.

This grants the emperor access to members' territory and members access to the emperor's territory. It does not grant direct access between two non-leader members.
