## Population evidence

- England and Wales uses a 2.2m target, with 2.01m in the English states. Broadberry et al. estimate about 1.9m in England around 1450 following the Black Death. Egypt also experienced repeated plague and agricultural contraction. The Egyptian target of 3.6m is a mid-fifteenth-century estimate informed by that contraction and Maddison's circa-1500 estimate of 4m. The choice puts Egypt above England and Wales and below France. [English tax-record reconstruction](https://academic.oup.com/jrssig/article/19/4/16/7029516), [Borsch on Mamluk irrigation and depopulation](https://www.scholarworks.wmich.edu/tmg/vol1/iss1/7/), [Maddison's population tables and regional discussions](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2006/09/the-world-economy_g1gh69e4/9789264022621-en.pdf).
- Maddison's circa-1500 estimates give the order of magnitude for the Eurasian and African groups. Early Ming household registers and later reconstructions diverge, so 85.5m is a working target between the early registered totals and higher estimates around 1500. Joseon uses 6.5m within the Korean historical synthesis's 5.5–7.5m range for the early dynasty. Most African, Central Asian and Southeast Asian allocations have weaker quantitative evidence, especially away from major river basins and urban centres. [Ming demographic evidence](https://www.lse.ac.uk/asset-library/information/wp219.pdf), [Korean National Institute of Korean History](https://contents.history.go.kr/front/ta/view.do?levelId=ta_h71_0060_0040_0010_0050), [Limits of historical population reconstructions](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022050723000293).
- The Americas sum to 52.06m. Koch et al.'s synthesis for 1492 estimates 60.5m, with an interquartile range of 44.8–78.2m. The mod uses a lower point within that range for 1444. The split favours central Mexican and Andean agricultural regions over later settlement centres. Population at the time of European contact is a proxy for regional scale. The difference between 52.06m and 60.5m is a modelling choice. [Koch et al., regional evidence and uncertainty](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quascirev.2018.12.004).
- Australian settlement estimates depend heavily on archaeological and demographic assumptions. The 0.8m target is at the lower end of the late pre-contact reconstruction used here. Aotearoa's 75,000 is a separate, low-confidence allocation, concentrated in the North Island. [Williams's Australian archaeological population curve](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23615287/), [existing Aotearoa and Pacific setup research](africa_nz_1444.md).

- The Nile Valley profiles give Copts about 10.6% of the population of the three core Egyptian states, with a greater share upstream. Medieval conversion studies support a substantial minority by the late Mamluk period. The regional gradient and state shares are modelling choices. The Coptic culture and Oriental Orthodox faith already exist in the mod. [Coptic conversion in Mamluk Egypt](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/record/749/files/MamlukStudiesReview_X-2_2006_14MB.pdf), [late Mamluk Coptic sources](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/record/740/files/MamlukStudiesReview_IX-2_2005.pdf).
- Valencia's Mudéjar share uses Meyerson's estimate of roughly 30% for the later fifteenth-century kingdom. The Andalusi profile retains an overwhelmingly Muslim Granada. The converter's widespread Sephardic and later Ashkenazi shares were reviewed against the expulsions, the geography of established communities and the late-medieval settlement chronology. The new Romaniote culture is used in the Byzantine sphere. Eastern Jewish is a game umbrella for several distinct Middle Eastern Jewish communities. [Meyerson on Valencia](https://isamveri.org/pdfdkm/13/DKM132415.pdf), [study of the English and French expulsions](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304418187900157), [Canarian conquest and population estimates](https://revistas-filologicas.unam.mx/medievalia/index.php/mv/article/view/374).
- The Balkan profiles use later fifteenth-century fiscal registers as evidence for surviving Christian communities, with local distinctions for Bosnia, Macedonia, and the Danube frontier. The Bohemian and Moravian Hussite splits are scenario estimates. The game groups several Hussite traditions under one existing faith. [Barkan's 1488–89 registers](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/belgeler/article/1826793), [Bosnian registers](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ehr.70022), [Dobrudja migration and settlement](https://hrcak.srce.hr/en/clanak/342790).
- The Levant adds a Maronite faith and represents documented Druze, Alawite, Orthodox, Syriac and Samaritan communities where relevant. Their shares have low quantitative confidence. The Iranian plateau profiles retain Shi'a pockets while reducing later Shi'a majorities in advance of Safavid state conversion after 1501. [Salibi on Mamluk-era Maronites](https://soas-repository.worktribe.com/output/397670/studies-on-the-traditional-historiography-of-the-maronites-on-the-period-1100-1516), [Iranian Studies on the Safavid confessional shift](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/iranian-studies/issue/C5F66F883038B4523675453A8BB58F81).
- West African Islamic shares distinguish trade centres from surrounding rural populations. In East Africa the coastal profiles represent the well-attested Swahili Muslim towns and preserve other communities inland. Beta Israel is represented in the Ethiopian highlands with a local culture and the existing Jewish faith. [Mali manuscript evidence](https://www.loc.gov/collections/islamic-manuscripts-from-mali/articles-and-essays/timbuktu-an-islamic-cultural-center/), [Swahili mosque archaeology](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/14696053231151667), [medieval Ethiopian archaeology](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/4F3CD1227F4A53E7732F4DAD9BE21F30/S0003598X20001684a.pdf/the-archaeology-of-complexity-and-cosmopolitanism-in-medieval-ethiopia-an-introduction.pdf).
- Eastern Bengal is smaller than western Bengal in this setup. Its Muslim population predates the major expansion of Muslim peasant settlement in the eastern delta, which Eaton dates mainly to later centuries. Melaka, Sulu and Champa receive mixed profiles to account for uneven adoption of Islam around ports and inland settlements. [Eaton on the Bengal frontier](https://www.ucpress.edu/books/the-rise-of-islam-and-the-bengal-frontier-1204-1760/paper), [fifteenth-century Melaka](https://academic.oup.com/isagsq/article/2/4/ksac072/6947856), [Philippine historical evidence](https://ncca.gov.ph/about-ncca-3/subcommissions/subcommission-on-cultural-communities-and-traditional-arts-sccta/central-cultural-communities/the-history-of-the-muslim-in-the-philippines/), [Cham religious history](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-southeast-asian-studies/article/praxis-and-policy-discourse-on-cham-bani-religious-identity-in-vietnam/06B490A27069397273F92ACC4A0E034D).
- The Americas remove post-contact Christian populations and represent Maya, Mesoamerican and Andean religions separately where the culture keys support that distinction. These are umbrella categories for diverse local practices. The Chimú culture covers the coastal population of the Chimor polity, with Quechua and other cultures in neighbouring territories. Jurchen, Indigenous Canarian, Romaniote, Eastern Jewish, Chimú and Beta Israel use existing related name pools or game assets as proxies where dedicated assets are absent. [Chimor archaeological assessment](https://whc.unesco.org/document/153276), [historical linguistics of the north Peruvian coast](https://journals.iai.spk-berlin.de/index.php/indiana/article/view/2278).

## Using the figures

Victoria 3 loads pops in state and owner scopes. A state with several owners can contain different local populations. Map provinces sharing an owner within a state share that demographic scope. Independent per-province populations would require changing the state map. The JSON report includes the map province identifiers for each split, along with headcounts and separate culture and religion percentages.

Explicit state totals take precedence within their regional budget. The remaining population follows inherited state weights as a proxy for the spatial distribution. Unprofiled owner splits use inherited cultural shares with period corrections. A small number of previously empty owner splits use province counts as an area proxy and get explicit compositions. The tool rounds allocations to whole people while preserving totals. Percentages express the scenario data to help reproduce the setup. Most local splits have no directly comparable fifteenth-century enumeration.

The Bermuda, Cape Verde and South Atlantic Islands state regions have zero permanent residents at the start. Their map owners remain in place for the existing game setup.

```powershell
node tools/population_1444.cjs
node tools/population_1444.cjs --report > population_1444.json
```

The first command validates coverage, ownership, keys, integer counts, regional totals and local profiles. To regenerate the active history from its input snapshot, run `node tools/population_1444.cjs --write --source-ref 00ee843`. This intentionally overwrites the four active pop files with the regenerated setup.
